Introduction

Bridge deck repairs are essential for ensuring the long-term safety and serviceability of transport infrastructure. The deterioration of concrete surfaces and reinforcement can lead to structural weaknesses, increased maintenance costs, and safety risks for road users.

Analysis and discussion

This section considers the causes of deck deterioration, common repair methods, and the importance of monitoring and maintenance planning. Effective repair strategies reduce life-cycle costs while improving resilience against environmental exposure and repeated traffic loading.
